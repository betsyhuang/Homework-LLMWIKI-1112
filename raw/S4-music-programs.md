# S4 — Music programs of South Korea

## Definitions
- Music program: weekly TV show where artists perform to promote songs; each has a chart/voting system that picks a weekly No.1 ("win").
- Debut stage: first performance on a show. Comeback stage: first performance of new songs, starting a promotional cycle.
- Triple crown: songs with 3 wins are excluded afterwards (some shows use 5).

## Weekly schedule
| Day | Show | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Tue | The Show | SBS FunE |
| Wed | Show Champion | MBC M |
| Thu | M Countdown | Mnet |
| Fri | Music Bank | KBS2 |
| Sat | Show! Music Core | MBC |
| Sun | Inkigayo | SBS |

## Scoring (as printed on page)
- Music Bank: digital 65, album 5, KBS broadcast 20, panel survey 10 (=100).
- Show Champion: digital 35, album 15, pre-vote 20, broadcast 20, SNS 10 (=100).
- M Countdown: digital 45, album 15, MV views 15, pre-vote 15, broadcast 10, live vote 10 (=110 as printed).
- Inkigayo: digital 55, SNS/YouTube 30, album 10, advance votes 5, on-air 10, live 5 (=115 as printed).

## Milestones
- 2012 Gangnam Style wins Music Bank 10 consecutive weeks (record; prior: Gee, 9 weeks, 2009).
- 2015 Girls' Generation first act with 100 music-show wins.
- 2020 BTS "Dynamite" 32 wins.

## Other
- Fans can attend recordings free by applying; no filming.
- 1998-06-16 Music Bank begins; 2004 M Countdown begins.
