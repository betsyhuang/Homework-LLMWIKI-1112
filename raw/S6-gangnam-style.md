# S6 — Gangnam Style (강남스타일)

- K-pop song by Psy, released 2012-07-15 via YG Entertainment; title refers to the lavish lifestyle of Seoul's Gangnam district. Signature "horse dance".
- 2012-09-01 most-viewed K-pop video on YouTube (passing "Gee").
- 2012-11-24 most-viewed YouTube video overall (passing "Baby").
- 2012-12-21 first YouTube video to reach 1 billion views.
- Billboard Hot 100 peak No.2 — then highest by a South Korean artist.
- Topped charts in 30+ countries by end of 2012.
- UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon called it "a force for world peace".
- [summary] UK No.1 on 2012-10-06.
- Page conflict: infobox says single from EP "Psy 6 (Six Rules), Part 1"; lead says lead single of 6th studio album.
